You may use the name "AgentPCAP" in text solely to refer to the open-source software project or its technology, provided that:
1. You do not imply official endorsement, partnership, or sponsorship by the AgentPCAP project.
2. You do not use the name or logos in a manner that creates confusion regarding the origin of your software or service.
3. Derivatives or distributions incorporating AgentPCAP must state clearly that they are built with or based upon AgentPCAP without claiming to be the official release.

- Do not register domain names, social media handles, or company names containing "AgentPCAP".
